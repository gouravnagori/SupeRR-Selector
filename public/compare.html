PI (Pressure Index)

A continuous pre-ball score from 0.0 to 1.0 combining 70% Leverage (expected swing in winning probability) and 30% State Tension (closeness to 50-50). High Pressure (HP) = top 25% highest-stakes balls.

RAE & RSAE

RAE (Runs Above Expected): Actual batter runs minus context expectation. RSAE (Runs Saved Above Expected): Expected bowler runs conceded minus actual runs conceded.

WP & WPA

WP (Win Probability): The instantaneous chance (0-100%) that the batting team wins. WPA (Win Probability Added): Change in win probability directly produced by the player on that delivery.

BPV & BoPV

BPV (Batting Pressure Value): Combined per-100-ball rating blending RAE, WPA, and dismissal survival. BoPV (Bowling Pressure Value): Combined bowling rating blending runs saved, wickets, and dot balls.

Hit Rate (% Consistency)

The percentage of high-pressure matches where the player beat match context expectation (net positive impact). Used to filter out erratic 'lucky one-off' performers.

95% Clustered CI

95% Confidence Interval: [Lower Bound, Upper Bound] calculated via 400-iteration Match-Clustered Block Bootstrap. A narrower range indicates a proven, high-certainty sample.
